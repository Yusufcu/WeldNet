About WeldNet

Project Overview

WeldNet is an open-source deep learning framework designed specifically for automated detection and classification of welding defects in industrial applications. The project aims to provide an accessible, efficient, and accurate solution for quality control in welding processes.

Why WeldNet?

Traditional welding inspection methods face several challenges:

  • Manual Inspection: Time-consuming, subjective, and requires skilled inspectors
  • X-ray/Ultrasonic Testing: Expensive equipment and specialized training required
  • Inconsistency: Human fatigue and subjectivity lead to variable results
  • Scalability: Difficult to inspect large volumes efficiently

WeldNet addresses these challenges by providing:

  • Automated, consistent defect detection
  • Real-time processing capability
  • Reduced dependency on specialized equipment
  • Scalable solution for high-volume inspection

Architecture Highlights

Depthwise Separable Convolutions

Reduces model parameters while maintaining performance, enabling faster inference and deployment on edge devices.

Attention Mechanisms

Improves the model's ability to focus on relevant features and defect patterns in welding images.

Residual Connections

Enables training of deeper networks and improves gradient flow for better convergence.

Research Background

WeldNet is inspired by recent advances in lightweight deep learning architectures for industrial inspection. The model draws from research on:

  • MobileNets and efficient CNN architectures
  • Attention mechanisms in computer vision
  • Domain-specific optimization for defect detection
  • Knowledge distillation and model compression
